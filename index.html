    Good to know

    Questions

    Which team am I on?

    If you work at Woodbridge, you’re . Everyone at Sterling Road (Pratus), Vancouver, Ottawa, North Bay and Timmins is . If you work remotely, join the team of the office you’re closest to.

    How can a smaller team win?

    Goals are $20 per person, so a 74-person team has a smaller goal than a 260-person one. The winner is whoever gets furthest past their own goal, not whoever raises the most dollars.

    What’s participation?

    It’s the share of each team’s people who donated any amount. Every gift counts, so even a few dollars helps your team’s number.

    Where does my donation go?

    Donations go through our Canada-wide food bank partner, supporting food banks in every community where we live and work.

    Do I get a tax receipt?

    Yes. Receipts come straight from our food bank partner, usually by email right after you give.

    How often is the scoreboard updated?

    Every Friday afternoon. Our partner tracks each team’s total on its own page, and we copy those totals here.

    Can I donate food instead of money?

    This year the drive is online donations only. Food banks can stretch every dollar further than a grocery donation, and it keeps the scoring simple.
