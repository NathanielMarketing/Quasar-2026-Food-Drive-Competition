    How it works

    Three steps, about two minutes

    1. Find your office

      Scroll to your office below. Each one has a single donation link to its local food bank.

    2. Donate online

      Give any amount through that link. The food bank sends your tax receipt directly.

    3. Check back Fridays

      Office champions report totals every Friday, and the leaderboard updates the same afternoon.

    The one rule: every office’s goal is $50 per person. The leaderboard ranks offices by how far they are toward that goal, so a 30-person office has the same shot at winning as a 120-person one.

      Good to know

      Questions

      Do I get a tax receipt?

      Yes. Receipts come straight from the food bank you donate to, usually by email right after you give.

      Can I donate to a different office’s food bank?

      You can, and your donation will count toward that office’s total. If you work remotely, pick the office you’re closest to.

      Why rank by % of goal instead of total dollars?

      Goals are based on headcount, so ranking by % of goal keeps it fair for smaller offices like North Bay and Timmins.

      Who reports my office’s total?

      Each office has a food drive champion who checks the donation page every Friday and sends the total in. Their name is on your office card.

      Can I donate food instead of money?

      This year the drive is online donations only. Food banks can stretch every dollar further than a grocery donation, and it keeps the scoring simple.
